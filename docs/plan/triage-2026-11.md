# Issue triage and plan, November 2026

Triage of every open issue on `main` at 95d0e12, on 2026-11-04. The backlog itself stays in [build plan section 10](build-plan.md#10-backlog). This page records what each issue is waiting on and the order to work in. Each issue's `status:*` label is the live version of the tables below.

## Where things stand

Since 2026-10-26, PRs #150 to #154 added about 67,000 lines of CPU-side work. That changes what limits progress. CPU work is well ahead of the GPU: 22 backlog items now have a CPU half and no GPU proof. Six things matter more than any single backlog item:

1. **The GPU path needs a rebuild ([#155](https://github.com/starlinghq/roost/issues/155)).** `kernels/manifest.toml` is at ABI 1.2 and contract version 3, while the loader is at ABI 1.3 and contract version 6. `KernelLibrary.Admit` refuses that manifest with `AssetMismatch`. CI can't see this: the validator only rejects a manifest newer than the header, and the GPU tests are outside the solution. This comes from reading the code, not from a GPU run. The last committed GPU results are from 2026-10-25.
2. **M0 waits on maintainer-only work.** `evidence/protected/` doesn't exist, so R-014 and R-022's fixtures and tolerances are proposals and nothing can reach `supported`. Contract versions 3 to 6 are also recorded as awaiting approval.
3. **No one has reviewed the recent merges ([#157](https://github.com/starlinghq/roost/issues/157)).** #150 to #154 have no recorded review, and CodeRabbit's runs failed. Those PRs include three contract versions.
4. **Starling has moved ([#156](https://github.com/starlinghq/roost/issues/156)).** Roost pins 0.3.0, and 0.5.0 is on nuget.org with a breaking package split. Doing this before the next evidence run means the evidence is produced once.
5. **Two issues are no longer blocked upstream.** #146 now needs only Roost's baseline SHA. #144 is unblocked for Qwen3.5 once #156 lands. #147 stays blocked, because `Starlings.Interventions` still isn't packable.
6. **One issue may be ready to close.** #77 (R-055) appears to meet its own acceptance criteria: its only open item is the M3 onboarding evidence, which is R-057's criterion. Its dependency #61 is still open, so closing it is left to the maintainer. #59 and #62 stay open only for GPU evidence, by their STATUS files. This triage closed nothing.

## The plan

**Stop starting new CPU halves until the GPU path is proven again.** Each one adds to the amount of unproven work, and most of the remaining risk is in the CUDA side.

### Round 1: make `main` trustworthy (no GPU)

1. #157: review the recent merges, one area at a time, and fix what it finds.
2. #158: settle the stress test, so CI results can be believed.
3. #146: adopt Starling's boundary scan, once the baseline SHA is set.
4. #156: raise the Starling pins, then #144 for the Qwen3.5 lock.
5. #52: finish the Qwen3-1.7B fixtures proposal, on the reference path.

### Round 2: restore the GPU path

6. #155: rebuild at ABI 1.3, tighten the validator, and rerun conformance, decode and chat on SM 86 and SM 89.

### Round 3: M0 and Preview 1

7. #51, then #55: adopt fixtures, rerun parity against them, and promote contracts to v1.
8. #82: use `KernelLibraryResolver` in `RoostWorkerRuntime.CommandFor`, which #153 left incomplete, then run the clean-consumer drill. #81's M0 half is the workflow this uses.

### Round 4: M1, GPU first

9. #56 (device side of async scheduling), then #57 (CUDA graphs) and #86 (recovery). These are the critical path to M1, and stream F is the bottleneck.
10. #64 (device sampler), since most sampling options are refused without it.
11. Stream A batches the contract work in one change: the mask table for #73, and the attention and fusion fields for #87, #65 and #93. #96 needs its params structs.
12. Run the first GPU end-to-end request through the server. #59 and #62 may need nothing more. #60, #75 and #90 still have named gaps: the engine's prefix-cache, preemption and queue-time metrics, the template corpus and Qwen3 tool support, and prompt-embeddings input.
13. #129 (window kernel), #61 (GPU gate harnesses), #69, then the M1 gate, #63.

M2 and later stay as the build plan orders them, and are worth re-planning at the M1 gate. At the recorded capacity the plan is schedulable to M3 only.
